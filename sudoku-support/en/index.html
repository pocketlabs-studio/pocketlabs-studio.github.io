Frequently asked questions

Q. I got a new device. How do I get my purchases back?

On a device signed in to the same Apple Account, you can tap “Restore purchases” on the “Worlds” screen. You can open “Worlds” from “Explore worlds” on Home.

Q. How do I move my records to a new device?

On your old device, you can save a records file with “Settings” → “Export records”. Then on your new device, you can pick that file in “Settings” → “Import records”. Puzzles in progress don't move over. Purchases don't move over. With the same store account, “Restore purchases” brings them back.

Q. I'd rather not send usage data.

If you turn off “Settings” → “Send usage data”, the app won't send usage stats or crash reports. You can turn it back on anytime. The Privacy Policy lists what gets sent.

Q. How do I get a refund?

App Store refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
